# Token art gaps

Tokens our cards create that have no set-scoped art, so they render with the
engine-wide generic art for their creature type. Generated by
`just token-art-gaps` — do not hand-edit.

To fill one in:

1. Save the art as `web-client/public/images/tokens/<file>.jpeg` (landscape, art only —
   the client frames it; a full card image gets cropped to its middle band).
2. Paste the `TokenPrinting(...)` row into that set's `tokenArt` in its `MtgSet`.

Sets missing from Scryfall's token sets entirely (`t<code>`) are the bulk of this:
Wizards printed no token cards before roughly Odyssey.
